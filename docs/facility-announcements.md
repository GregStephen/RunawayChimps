Confirmed optional prototype choice: September 30, 2026. Branch: `feature/facility-announcements`, based on freshly fetched main `930a8f830cae99e06dcaa0a78ab9edc968c42ae5`. This is not a launch requirement. Its implementation does not depend on the toys, regeneration-lab art kit or security-console branches.

## Status and boundaries

Implemented source and committed serialized content: one industrial speaker prefab, one local-camera caption prefab, four Standard materials, a four-entry collection, four prerecorded native-formant synthetic speech WAVs, two original soft relay/chime WAVs, metadata, Inspector controls, explicit Editor tools and feature-local managed policy tests. Human listening, intelligibility and comfort acceptance are still pending.

## Exact setup

Open this branch in Unity 2022.3.62f3. Let scripts and audio import. Outside Play Mode, choose **Tools > Runaway Chimps > Atmosphere > Place Facility Speaker in Hub**. The command opens Hub_Base additively only when needed, instantiates the real prefab near the existing Hub arrival marker, selects it and marks Hub dirty. It supports Undo. It does not remove or reposition existing speakers, rewrite Hub/Bootstrap, or save the scene. Move and rotate the new prefab root to your chosen wall; its front grille faces local +Z. Save your placement manually when satisfied.

The initial anchor-relative position is `(1.5, 2.1, 2.5)` metres and faces back toward the arrival marker. This is a testing placement, not a surveyed final wall position. The approximately 58 cm wide housing has a recessed grille, nine louvers, screw heads, an identification plate and rear mounting lugs. It has no gameplay collider.

Start the game from the existing **Bootstrap** scene and enter a Photon room. Opening Hub directly without the existing travel/local-rig services intentionally does not start scheduling. No automatic placement is performed during editor load or Play Mode.

## Preview and diagnostic controls

**Tools > Runaway Chimps > Atmosphere > Facility Announcement Preview** selects a collection entry, shows its transcript and provenance status, and auditions its actual recorded speech outside Play Mode. Editor audition is non-spatial and uses Unity's Editor preview API, with a guarded fallback message when unavailable. It stops on window focus loss, close, timeout or Play Mode changes. The separately labelled **TEXT / START-CUE PREVIEW - not a voiced announcement** is development-only and never enters the normal network schedule.

Enable **Diagnostic scheduling** in that window or the collection Inspector for testing. It is honored only in the Editor or a development build. Default diagnostic intervals are **8-12 seconds**, with a conservative **36-second authority-settling/handover guard** before the first announcement. The guard prevents a new authority from starting on top of an old controller's bounded clip. A release player ignores the diagnostic switch. Turn it off before normal atmosphere evaluation.

Normal quiet intervals default to **180-300 seconds after an announcement**, not a constant stream. Normal settings have a 60-second minimum and 1,800-second ceiling; diagnostic intervals have a 5-second minimum. Invalid/reversed settings are clamped. A failed selection or a rejected event enqueue waits another full quiet interval rather than repeatedly logging or firing empty events. A rejected enqueue does not consume repeat history. A fresh authority check is performed immediately before a due send, rather than relying only on the periodic membership poll.

Run **Tools > Runaway Chimps > Atmosphere > Validate Facility Announcement Assets** after imports/content changes. Run **Tools > Runaway Chimps > Atmosphere > Run Facility Announcement Regression Checks** outside Play Mode and disconnected from Photon for the isolated collection, caption-cleanup and prefab/source checks. The latter uses temporary test clips only inside its test fixture; these are never recorded content or normal announcement assets. These Editor checks are authored, not executed by source CI.

## Tuning and local subtitles

Keep every speaker in one sector on the same collection asset and content revision. Set its `sector` to match the existing SectorScene. Multiple placements are allowed; there is one schedule and at most one output source per client. At announcement start the nearest eligible speaker is chosen, avoiding summed loudness or phase doubling from overlapping placements. It remains the origin for that announcement; walking to another speaker does not restart or duplicate it. This prototype does not model wall occlusion.

Speaker defaults are volume **0.38**, cue multiplier **0.55**, linear 3D attenuation, **1.5 m** minimum distance, **14 m** maximum distance, no Doppler or random speech pitch, and lower-priority environmental playback. A hard 0.7 volume cap limits accidental loud settings. Nonfinite volume/distance values use safe defaults; source minimum distance is bounded to 0.1-100 m and maximum distance to at least minimum + 0.1 m and at most 1,000 m. These are defensive limits, not recommended atmospheric tuning. Nonfinite subtitle scale/height preferences also return to safe defaults. Optional `outputGroup` can use an existing appropriate environmental mixer group; never route to Photon Voice or change other audio systems. Evaluate final loudness against footsteps, interactions and critical sounds on the headset.

Captions attach to the existing local XROrigin camera as a noninteractive ScreenSpaceCamera Canvas. They appear only while the selected speech AudioSource is observed playing and the listener is within that source's maximum distance. No captions during cues, missing speech, normal silence or travel. The text is captured with the selected clip, so live transcript edits cannot change an already playing line. Externally stopping/replacing the clip, disabling the output or caption view, pause/focus loss, room changes and travel cancel/reset presentation. Disabled caption components reject direct presentation calls and cannot retain hidden text for a later re-enable. The playback slot has an explicit expiry: a stalled client cancels expired audio and never starts delayed speech that cannot finish within the remaining slot. It does not replay or try to catch up after a stall. The view is reused rather than instantiated every announcement.

The preview window exposes **local captions**, **local text scale (0.8-1.5)** and **local caption height (0.18-0.42)**. These use PlayerPrefs on that machine, not Photon room properties. The runtime API is `FacilityAnnouncementCaptions.CaptionsEnabled`, `.TextScale`, and `.VerticalPosition`; a future existing settings UI can call those without a new camera or network feature. This prototype does not add a new in-headset settings menu. The caption prefab uses the existing LiberationSans SDF asset and a dark background. Readability in both eyes, longer authored lines and large-font comfort must be checked on device; source checks cannot establish that.

## Content and provenance

These are **proposed, editable prototype lines**, not new canonical staff identities, experiment histories, evacuation events or monster origins.

| Stable ID | Matching spoken transcript |
| --- | --- |
| `enrichment-privilege` | Reminder: enrichment equipment is a privilege, not an escape opportunity. |
| `tissue-movement` | Please report unexpected tissue movement to your supervisor. |
| `assigned-containment` | Unauthorized subjects must return to their assigned containment area. |
| `cooperation-recorded` | Your cooperation has been recorded. Your objections have not. |

The four source texts were supplied by Greg for this prototype. Speech was rendered offline using **native eSpeak 1.48.15 `en-us`** with `-s 145 -p 38 -a 85`, then saved as mono 22,050 Hz, 16-bit PCM WAV. There is no cloned or named person's voice, MBROLA database, purchased recording, cloud speech account or runtime synthesizer. Light 140 Hz high-pass / 4,400 Hz low-pass processing, peak normalization to 0.65 and endpoint fades provide restrained speaker coloration without added static over speech. This is numeric processing evidence, not a listening approval.

`relay-start.wav` and `relay-end.wav` are original 0.22-second nonverbal sine/decay cues generated by the checked-in Python authoring code. They are not spoken announcements. The generator records exact filenames, command, synthesizer version, durations and SHA-256 hashes in `Audio/provenance.json`. The original cue waveforms, housing arrangement and materials are newly authored for this feature. No third-party sound recording was downloaded or sampled. Existing Unity primitives, Standard shader, uGUI and the repository's TMP font remain under their existing licenses; no font files are added by this feature.

[eSpeak's official documentation](https://espeak.sourceforge.net/) identifies it as an open-source GPL speech synthesizer using formant synthesis, and its [command reference](https://espeak.sourceforge.net/commands.html) documents WAV output. The tool is used only for offline authoring; its executable, libraries and voice-data files are not shipped in the game or this change. [GNU GPL v3 section 2](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html#section2) distinguishes running a tool from licensing output according to its content. The delivered clips are synthesized renditions of the supplied lines, not a redistribution of the synthesizer. Retain this provenance when replacing/exporting clips and document the actual license of any future externally sourced recording rather than inheriting this description.

For a new entry, create a permanent lowercase/digit/hyphen/underscore ID (maximum 64 characters), assign its actual speech recording, enter the matching transcript, record its origin/permission, and mark `speechAvailable` only when speech exists. Clips must be preloaded, non-streaming, longer than 0.05 seconds and at most 30 seconds; transcripts must be nonempty and at most 240 characters. Prefer a short sentence comparable to the four supplied lines; check long text at maximum local scale rather than assuming it fits. Increase `contentRevision` whenever changing clip/transcript associations and deploy matching content to all clients. The collection is capped at 32 entries. Cue clips over one second or unavailable audio are ignored.

Use `enabled` and integer weight 1-10 for simple selection. A zero/negative weight is treated as unavailable by both scheduling and receive-side content lookup, not merely by random selection. Streaming clips cannot satisfy the loaded speech/cue contract. Immediate repeats are excluded by stable ID. With only one playable entry, default behavior is one announcement then silence; opt into `allowSingleLineRepeat` only deliberately. Duplicate IDs reject both entries, including a pending duplicate. Empty collections, disabled entries, null clips, pending speech and clips whose audio data failed to load are skipped without recurring errors or unexplained caption-only normal playback.

Regeneration is optional development work: `python Tools/FacilityAnnouncements/build_assets.py` requires locally installed native eSpeak and Python, not a paid service. Delivered assets are already committed and need no generator to run in Unity. The tool refuses to overwrite existing generated prefabs without `--replace-generated`. That option replaces the original asset files, so review the diff carefully; ordinary scene placement/content edits should be made in Unity instead. It never edits Hub, Bootstrap, other features or project settings.

## Multiplayer and lifecycle contract

The existing `SectorPresence` rules identify sector membership. A player additionally advertises this feature's loaded-speaker/local-rig readiness using `rc.fa.sector` and a fresh `rc.fa.ticket`. The lowest eligible actor in that sector owns scheduling, even when the Master Client is in a different unloaded environment. The readiness ticket changes on each room/visit/resume generation. Presence refresh is bounded to four times per second; cancellation checks run locally every frame without scene-wide searches. Rig resolution uses the existing Gorilla player/XROrigin and retries at most once per second when absent. A disabled current Gorilla player invalidates readiness immediately, even when its previously cached camera remains enabled. Replacing the current rig invalidates that camera cache and binds the replacement through the same local-marker/ownership checks.

Event **197**, protocol `rc.facility.1`, carries only bounded collection/revision/ID, authority ticket, serial, issue time, duration and targeted actor/visit tickets. It uses reliable uncached RaiseEvent, targeted only at ready peers in the sector, including the sender. The sender follows the same receive path as listeners. Receivers re-evaluate authority and reject wrong sector, revision, actor/ticket, duplicate/out-of-order serial, stale/future timestamp, malformed payload or an occupied slot. A new arrival is not in an old target-ticket snapshot and simply waits for the next announcement; there is no request/replay backlog. The room holds only one bounded last-selected ID per supported sector (`rc.fa.last.1` through `.3`) for repeat prevention across handover, not a queue or timer.

A handover cancels local playback and preserves the accepted active-slot guard, then starts a fresh quiet interval with a 36-second safety floor. No old announcement is resumed. A room-object or actor change clears room-specific history/gates. At Photon Time's unsigned-millisecond rollover, old deadlines and tickets are retired and playback is cancelled; same-room repeat history is preserved and a fresh quiet guard is armed. This is a feature-local conservative reset, not a replacement network clock or shared scheduling framework. Travel, scene replacement, pause, focus loss, timeScale zero, AudioListener pause and disabling the active speaker cancel sound/captions and withdraw readiness. Resume creates a new ticket and a fresh quiet wait rather than catching up. Disabled/destroyed speaker instances unregister. There is one bounded local director, one reused caption view, at most 16 registered speakers and no per-announcement source allocation. There is no new general networking framework.

This is a cooperative atmosphere protocol, not a security boundary against a modified client. Malformed/stale/duplicate traffic is filtered, but authoritative Photon transport and lifecycle ordering must still be exercised with real clients.
